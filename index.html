01 Take City fixtures out +
Every fixture involving Manchester City becomes a blank gameweek for both sides. We remove all points from that match: goals, assists, clean sheets, saves, bonus and defensive contributions, plus any negative points. Points from other fixtures in a double gameweek stay.
02 Pass the armband properly +
If your captain has no appearance in a remaining fixture, captaincy passes to an eligible vice-captain. Triple Captain passes with it. If neither qualifies, there’s no captain bonus and the chip stays used. A captain who plays another fixture in the same gameweek keeps the armband.
03 Bring the bench into play +
We restore your original starting XI and bench order, then replay automatic substitutions. Replacements must play in a remaining fixture and keep a legal formation. Goalkeepers only replace goalkeepers. Bench Boost still counts all 15 players’ eligible points.
04 Keep your decisions intact +
Your squad, transfers, transfer hits, chip choices and bench order stay as they were. We recalculate every scored gameweek from the league’s start. Equal point totals are separated by fewer transfers, excluding Wildcard and Free Hit transfers. This is a scoring what-if, not a prediction of how you’d manage a season without City.
05 Be clear about live gameweeks +
A gameweek in progress is marked provisional. We count points already earned and project automatic substitutions. Players with an unplayed remaining fixture provisionally keep their starting spot, bench priority and captaincy. Final appearances, bonus points and FPL corrections can change the result. Published FPL totals may also lag projected substitutions.

Works with classic mini-leagues of up to 200 teams. You need the numeric league ID, not an invite code. No FPL login needed. FPL scoring rules ↗
